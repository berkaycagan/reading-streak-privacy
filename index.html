This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the Reading Streak app for Android and iOS (“the app”). In short: your reading data stays on your phone. There is no account, no tracking and no advertising in the current version of the app.

2. Data stored only on your device

The app saves the following information locally on your phone, in the app's own storage:

  • the books you add (title, author, number of pages, cover link, current page),
  • your reading entries (date, time and number of pages),
  • your notes and book ratings,
  • your settings (daily goal, yearly goal, reminder time, earned badges).

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data. Please note that because the data exists only on your device, we cannot restore it if your phone is lost or reset.

3. Book search (Open Library)

When you search for a book, the app sends your search words to Open Library, a free book database run by the Internet Archive (a non-profit organisation in the USA), to show matching books. Book covers are loaded from Open Library as well. As with any internet request, Open Library receives your device's IP address and the search words. We do not receive or store these. The search only happens when you use it.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the search function you request). For Open Library's handling of data, see the Internet Archive's terms and privacy policy. Because the Internet Archive is located in the USA, data may be processed outside the EU.

4. Reminders

Daily reminders are created on your phone by the app itself. No data is sent anywhere to show them. You can turn reminders off in the app's settings or in your phone's notification settings.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet – for the book search and cover images.
  • Notifications – for the daily reading reminder.
  • Run at startup – so reminders are kept after your phone restarts.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. As your reading data is stored only on your device, you can view, change or delete it directly in the app. For any question, contact us at the email above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority of the German state where you live.

8. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes, for example if optional paid features or optional ads are added. The date at the top shows the latest version.
